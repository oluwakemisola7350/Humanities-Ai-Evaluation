## Evaluation Questions

This project uses four evaluation questions designed to test an AI model’s ability to reason about evidence concerning Nigeria’s fuel-price reforms, inflation, macroeconomic conditions, and household welfare.

The questions focus on four capabilities:

1. Causal reasoning
2. Interpretation of statistical evidence
3. Source comparison and contextual reasoning
4. Understanding competing economic mechanisms

The questions are based on evidence documented in sources/sources.md.

⸻

Question 1 — Causal Reasoning

Prompt

Reuters reports on the removal of Nigeria’s fuel subsidy and subsequent increases in petrol prices, transportation costs, and household expenses. Using the Reuters report and the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) data, evaluate whether the available evidence is sufficient to conclude that the removal of the fuel subsidy directly caused Nigeria’s subsequent inflation.

In your answer:

* distinguish between temporal association, a plausible contributing mechanism, and evidence sufficient to establish causation;
* identify at least two alternative factors that could have contributed to inflation; and
* support your reasoning with evidence from the cited sources.

Primary skills tested

* Causal reasoning
* Evidence evaluation
* Alternative explanations
* Source use
* Analytical writing

Relevant sources

* Reuters — August 8, 2024
* NBS Consumer Price Index
* NBS Premium Motor Spirit Price Watch

⸻

Question 2 — Interpreting Inflation Data

Prompt

The National Bureau of Statistics reported that Nigeria’s headline inflation rate was 15.06% year-on-year in February 2026, compared with 26.27% in February 2025. February 2026 month-on-month inflation was 2.01%.

Explain why these figures are not contradictory.

What would be an incorrect conclusion to draw from the decline in the year-on-year inflation rate, and what additional information does the month-on-month figure provide?

Use the NBS data to support your explanation.

Primary skills tested

* Statistical interpretation
* Factual accuracy
* Conceptual reasoning
* Precision
* Evidence use

Relevant sources

* NBS Consumer Price Index — February 2026

⸻

Question 3 — Macroeconomic Conditions and Household Welfare

Prompt

The World Bank’s April 2026 Nigeria Development Update describes improvements in Nigeria’s macroeconomic stability while also noting that household incomes had not fully recovered and that poverty remained high.

Explain how these observations can coexist.

Why would it be insufficient to use GDP growth or a lower inflation rate alone to conclude that household living standards have improved?

Identify additional indicators that should be considered when assessing changes in household welfare, and explain why they are relevant.

Primary skills tested

* Contextual reasoning
* Interpretation of economic indicators
* Distinguishing macroeconomic performance from household welfare
* Evidence-based reasoning
* Analytical writing

Relevant sources

* World Bank — Nigeria Development Update, April 2026
* NBS Consumer Price Index
* IMF — 2026 Article IV Consultation with Nigeria

⸻

Question 4 — Competing Economic Mechanisms

Prompt

The IMF explains that higher global fuel, food, and fertilizer prices can affect Nigeria through multiple channels, including effects on exports and government revenues as well as inflationary pressures and risks to poverty and food security.

Explain how these apparently opposing effects can occur simultaneously.

Identify the different economic channels involved and explain why describing such a price shock as simply “good” or “bad” for Nigeria would provide an incomplete analysis.

Support your explanation with evidence from the IMF report and at least one other source in this project.

Primary skills tested

* Multi-step reasoning
* Economic mechanism analysis
* Source interpretation
* Contextual understanding
* Avoidance of oversimplification
* Evidence use

Relevant sources

* IMF — 2026 Article IV Consultation with Nigeria
* World Bank — Nigeria Development Update, April 2026
* NBS Consumer Price Index
* NBS Premium Motor Spirit Price Watch

⸻

Evaluation Design

Each question is designed to require more than factual recall. A strong response should accurately represent the sources, distinguish evidence from inference, avoid unsupported causal claims, and explain relevant context.

The model responses will be evaluated using the criteria documented in evaluation/rubric.md.
