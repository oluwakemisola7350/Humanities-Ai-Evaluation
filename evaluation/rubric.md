Evaluation Rubric

Purpose: 

This rubric is used to evaluate AI-generated responses to questions about Nigeria’s fuel-price reforms, inflation, macroeconomic conditions, and household welfare.
The evaluation focuses on whether the model can reason from evidence, distinguish facts from inference, interpret sources accurately, and respond to nuanced questions without making unsupported claims.
Evaluation Criteria

1. Factual Accuracy
Does the response accurately represent the information contained in the provided sources?

Look for:
* Correct facts, figures, dates, and relationships.
* No contradictions with the cited sources.
* No misleading descriptions of statistical data.

2. Causal Reasoning
Does the response distinguish between correlation, temporal association, plausible mechanisms, and established causation?

Look for:

* Recognition that events occurring sequentially do not automatically establish causation.
* Consideration of alternative explanations.
* Appropriate treatment of evidence limitations.
* Avoidance of unsupported causal claims.
* 
3. Evidence and Source Grounding
Does the response support its claims with the sources provided in the prompt?

Look for:

* Appropriate use of the cited sources.
* Specific evidence where relevant.
* Clear connection between claims and supporting sources.
* No unnecessary reliance on information outside the provided sources.
  
4. Distinguishing Evidence from Inference
Does the response clearly distinguish what the sources directly establish from what the model is inferring?

Look for:

* Explicit identification of inference.
* Appropriate use of cautious language when evidence is incomplete.
* No presentation of interpretation as established fact.

5. Contextual and Analytical Reasoning
Does the response consider the broader context needed to interpret the evidence?

Look for:

* Recognition of multiple economic or social mechanisms where relevant.
* Understanding of how different indicators can move in different directions.
* Avoidance of overly simple explanations for complex outcomes.

6. Instruction Adherence
Does the response follow the requirements of the question and evaluation prompt?

Look for:

* Answers all parts of the question.
* Uses only the permitted sources when instructed.
* Follows requested distinctions or comparisons.
* Does not omit important requested elements.

7. Clarity and Analytical Precision
Is the reasoning clearly expressed and appropriately precise?

Look for:

* Logical organization.
* Clear explanations of complex concepts.
* Precise terminology.
* No unnecessary repetition or vague claims.
* Evaluation Approach
* Each response should be evaluated against all criteria above.
* The evaluation should document:
* Specific strengths demonstrated by the response.
* Specific weaknesses or limitations.
* Claims that require stronger source support.
* Any factual inaccuracies or misleading statements.
* Whether the response followed the instructions given to the model.
* The evaluation should focus on observable characteristics of the response and its relationship to the provided evidence rather than on general impressions of the model.
